📧 Showing the QR inside emails

The QR above is what customers see on the confirmation page. Emails need a second copy, because Gmail strips embedded images — an emailed QR has to be a normal web address.

  1. Save your QR image as gcash-qr.png
  2. Upload it to your GitHub repo, in the same folder as index.html
  3. Open the link below — if the QR appears, your emails will show it too
https://interptools.github.io/arroyo-cv-builder/gcash-qr.png ↗

Until that link works, emails simply show the GCash number on its own — nothing breaks, and customers can still pay.

🧪 Send a Test Email

Sends a real "CV is Ready" email to yourself using the exact same template, variables and GCash block your customers receive. The fastest way to check a template change before a customer sees it — and to confirm EmailJS is working at all.

A test uses one of your monthly allowance and appears in EmailJS → Email History like any other send. The subject is prefixed [TEST] so you can tell it apart. No customer record is touched.
