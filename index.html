Language Levels

European employers expect CEFR grades, not "fluent" or "conversational"

Quick guide to CEFR
A1/A2 — basic phrases and everyday exchanges · B1 — can handle most work situations with effort · B2 — comfortable in meetings and technical discussion; the usual minimum asked for in job adverts · C1 — fluent and precise, including nuance · C2 — equivalent to an educated native speaker. Filipino applicants are usually safe putting English at C1 or C2.
List your strongest language first. If you hold a certificate (Goethe, TELC, NT2, IELTS, TOEFL), put it in the certificate field — it carries far more weight than a self-assessment.
