Questions

Which one should I pick?

If you are comfortable typing your own work history, build it yourself — it costs less and you can change it as often as you like. If your history is long or you would rather someone else handle the wording and layout, send your details instead.

Can I switch after I start?

Yes. Nothing is charged until you pay, so you can try building your own and send your details instead if you change your mind.

I am a seafarer. Which route?

Either. Both carry the maritime layouts with travel documents, STCW certificates and a sea service table. Building it yourself means typing every certificate and vessel in yourself, which is the longest part of a seaman CV.

I am applying to Europe or the Gulf.

Both routes have formats built for these. The Europe layouts leave out your photo, date of birth and civil status unless you ask for them, and use CEFR language levels. The Gulf layouts put passport, visa status and availability at the top, which is what agencies there check first.

How long does it take?

Building it yourself is as fast as you can type — you can pay and print within the hour. If I make it for you, it depends on how many orders are ahead of yours; message the GCash number above if you need it urgently.
